Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about reserving study rooms, passcode entry, and library rules.

How do I access my reserved room?

When you complete a booking on your dashboard, a unique 4-digit PIN is generated. Type this code into the electronic door keypad at your designated start time.

Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?

Yes! You can cancel or modify any upcoming reservation directly from your Member Dashboard up to 1 hour prior to your session start time without losing credits.

What are the silence rules in the Solo Pods?

Solo pods are strictly zero-decibel environments. Phone calls must be conducted in the building atrium, and all audio devices must be used with headphones.

Are food and drinks permitted inside the pods?

Lidded beverages (water, coffee, tea) are welcome. Hot meals or odorous snacks are restricted to the ground floor lounge area.
