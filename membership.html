Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about membership tiers, room reservations, passcode entry, and library etiquette.

How do I access my reserved room or silent pod?
When you complete a room booking on your Member Dashboard, a unique 4-digit PIN is generated. Simply enter this code on the electronic door keypad at your designated start time.
Can I cancel or reschedule a booking without losing credits?
Yes! You can cancel or reschedule any upcoming reservation directly from your Member Dashboard up to 1 hour prior to your session start time without incurring any penalty or losing credits.
What are the silence rules and decibel standards in Solo Pods?
Solo pods are strictly zero-decibel environments. Phone calls must be conducted in the ground floor atrium, audio devices must be used with headphones, and acoustic compliance is continuously monitored.
Are food and beverages permitted inside the study suites?
Lidded beverages (water, coffee, tea) are fully welcome. Hot meals or odorous snacks are restricted to the ground-floor member lounge area to preserve pristine air quality.
How does the 24/7 keycard entry work for Scholar & Resident Fellow plans?
Active Scholar Pro and Resident Fellow members receive smartphone NFC access keys and physical RFID keycards, enabling full 24/7 entry through the main building entrance and pod corridors at any hour.
